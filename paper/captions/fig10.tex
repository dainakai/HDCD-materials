Representative visually confirmed collision--coalescence events reconstructed using three phase-retrieval iterations. Cases (a) and (b) are the author-selected examples from raw sequences S0015 (review rank 1) and S0022 (rank 2). Each case shows five reconstructed images and the corresponding smoothed three-dimensional trajectories. Red and blue identify terminating and continuing tracks; triangles and squares mark the starts and ends of the displayed trajectory segments. Open circles and short lines overlay observed particle positions and recent track histories. Image times are relative to raw candidate frames 6750 and 4087, respectively, with offsets of -2, -1, 0, +1 and +2 ms at 4000 frames per second. The displayed square fields are 0.96 mm in (a) and 1.20 mm in (b), cropped for display from unchanged 128-by-128-pixel reconstructions at 10 micrometres per pixel. Each case uses one fixed field width across its five images. Headings give the terminating-parent, continuing-parent and post-event median diameters: 127.9, 182.4 and 201.2 \(\mu\mathrm{m}\) in (a), and 99.5, 211.3 and 217.5 \(\mu\mathrm{m}\) in (b). Scale bars represent 500 \(\mu\mathrm{m}\). The axial coordinate is depth relative to the reconstruction front.

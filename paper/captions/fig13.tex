Physical-consistency diagnostics for reviewed experimental candidates, excluding ambiguous judgments. (a) Measured post-event diameter versus the volume-conservation prediction, \(D_{\mathrm{exp}}=(D_1^3+D_2^3)^{1/3}\). (b) Measured downward-positive vertical velocity versus the mass-weighted prediction, \(v_{y,\mathrm{exp}}=(D_1^3v_{y,1}+D_2^3v_{y,2})/(D_1^3+D_2^3)\). Orange markers show 184 confirmed events with valid diameter measurements and 179 with valid velocity estimates. Blue hexagonal bins show 908 and 822 noncollision candidates, respectively, on logarithmic color scales. Diameter windows contain up to the last ten observations at or before raw candidate frame \(f-2\) and the first twenty after \(f\). Velocities are medians of observed displacement-to-frame-interval ratios within these windows; each velocity window requires at least three observations. Two confirmed events lack a required diameter window, and seven fail the velocity-window requirements. The corresponding counts for noncollision candidates are 14 and 100. Missing measurements or insufficient observed points exclude a candidate only from the affected diagnostic; one observed point may supply a diameter but cannot define an interval velocity. No residual-based outlier removal is applied. Black lines show one-to-one agreement.

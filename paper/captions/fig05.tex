Reprocessed diameter--velocity distributions for 72,047 trajectories with at least five valid observations and median diameters between 25 and 300 \(\mu\mathrm{m}\). Each trajectory contributes its median diameter and median smoothed velocity components. (a) Horizontal, (b) downward-positive vertical, and (c) axial velocity. The dashed curve is the Beard (1976) terminal velocity minus the unchanged upward-flow offset of 0.26 \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\). The axes show the same ranges as the earlier figure; trajectories outside these display ranges are retained in the underlying summaries. These measurements do not redefine the frozen synthetic training distribution.

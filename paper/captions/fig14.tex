Confirmed collision--coalescence counts and volumetric rates compared with gravitational-kernel predictions, separately for 14 sequences and for their pooled result. Open circles show the 186 visually confirmed events without correction for missed events; filled diamonds show predictions from the reprocessed frame-wise number-density distributions. All 10,917 frames per sequence and the nominal 10.24-by-10.24-by-102.3 \(\mathrm{mm^3}\) volume are used. Error bars show conditional Poisson standard deviations, \(\sqrt{\Lambda}\), with density and kernel fixed. The pooled prediction is \(222.41\pm14.91\) events. Secondary axes give counts corresponding to the indicated exposure. The bars do not describe uncertainty from measured diameters, density estimation, model choice or missed events.

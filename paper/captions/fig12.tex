Retrospective confirmed-event coverage and review precision versus the number of candidates reviewed per sequence. Candidates are ordered by final score separately within each of the 14 sequences. Coverage is the fraction of the 186 events confirmed within the top-80 cohort; precision uses all reviewed candidates, including ambiguous judgments, in its denominator. The upper axis gives the total review count. No equal integer budget gives exactly 50\% precision: 24 candidates per sequence give 173 confirmed events among 336 reviews (51.49\% precision), and 25 give the same 173 among 350 reviews (49.43\%). Both recover 93.01\% of the confirmed-event set. The two vertical lines mark 24 candidates per sequence and the smallest per-sequence budget attaining 95\% coverage, 34: 177 events among 476 reviews, or 95.16\% coverage and 37.18\% precision. Coverage is conditional on the reviewed confirmed-event set and is not absolute experimental recall.
